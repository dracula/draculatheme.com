## New on Dracula

Meet the 10 new additions to the clan:

- [Claude Code](/claude-code-cli): Anthropic's **agentic coding tool** for terminal workflows.
- [tuicr](/tuicr): a **keyboard-first terminal interface** for reviewing Git diffs.
- [Paseo](/paseo): a desktop app for **running and managing coding agents**.
- [Google Colab](/google-colab): Google's **hosted Jupyter Notebook environment**.
- [SpeedGrid](/speedgrid): a **customizable browser dashboard** for bookmarks and shortcuts.
- [lazydocker](/lazydocker): a simple **terminal interface for Docker** containers and services.
- [ruTorrent](/rutorrent): a **browser interface for rTorrent**.
- [Astryx](/astryx): a **design system for React** projects powered by StyleX.
- [Antinote](/antinote): a **lightweight scratchpad for macOS**.
- [Wireshark](/wireshark): a **network protocol analyzer** for troubleshooting and exploration.
