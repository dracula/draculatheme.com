The clan keeps growing. This round we have **9 new themes** from the community, and the rebuilt **Firefox** and **Notepad++** themes are finally official on Dracula Pro.

## New on Dracula

Meet the 9 new additions:

- [Telegram X](/telegram-x): an alternative **Telegram client for Android**, built for speed.
- [SwayOSD](/swayosd): a GTK **on-screen display** for volume, brightness, and caps lock.
- [Neiki's Page Editor](/neiki-page-editor): a visual **HTML page editor** with browser-accurate rendering.
- [SecureCRT](/securecrt): a rock-solid **SSH and Telnet client** for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- [Starlight](/astro-starlight): Astro's official **documentation framework**.
- [alphai-tui](/alphai-tui): a **terminal stock dashboard** with live quotes and AI-scored news.
- [StationView](/stationview): live **train departure boards** for stations around the world.
- [Kali Browser](/kali-browser): the default **web browser** on Kali Linux.
- [T3 Code](/t3code): an open-source **GUI for coding agents** like Claude Code and Codex.

[![A terminal stock dashboard displaying stock symbols, prices, and news headlines](/images/blog/t3-code-telegram-plus-7-new-community-additions-a.webp)](/alphai-tui)

## New on Dracula Pro

The rebuilt [Firefox](https://github.com/dracula-pro/firefox) and [Notepad++](https://github.com/dracula-pro/notepad-plus-plus) themes are **out of beta**. They now ship in the official Dracula Pro package on Gumroad.

If you already have Pro, download the latest version from your [Gumroad library](https://app.gumroad.com/library). New here? [Get Dracula Pro](/pro).
